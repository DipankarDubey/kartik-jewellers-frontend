Customer account privacy

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, we use the name, verified email and Google account identifier shared during sign-in. We use these details to identify your account and connect it to your existing jewellery plans. You can add a phone number yourself in your profile. We also keep your jewellery plan, installment and payment records.

How we use it

Store administrators can access account contact details and payment records to assist customers and manage their plans. We use hosting and service providers to operate the site, deliver email and process online payments. We discard Google tokens after sign-in.

Promotional email

We send jewellery offers by email when you opt in. You can change this choice in your profile or use the unsubscribe link in each promotional email.

Access, correction and deletion

You can update your name and phone in your profile. To ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion, contact Karthik Jewellers. We retain account information to provide your plan and related support and may retain records needed for payments or other applicable obligations.
