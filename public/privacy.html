Customer account privacy

Information we collect

We collect your name, mobile number and email when you register or when the showroom sets up your account. Your mobile number is your username. We store a password hash and use your email to recover access and to verify that you own an existing customer record when linking it. We also keep your jewellery plan, installment and payment records. When you place an order, we keep the products you purchased and the delivery details you provide so we can fulfill it.

How we use it

Store administrators can access account contact details and payment records to assist customers and manage their plans. We use hosting and service providers to operate the site, deliver email and process online payments. We use your account email to send delivery updates and use your delivery details to fulfill your order.

Promotional email

We send jewellery offers by email when you opt in. You can change this choice in your profile or use the unsubscribe link in each promotional email.

Access, correction and deletion

You can update your name in your profile. Contact the showroom if you need to update your sign-in mobile number or email. To ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion, contact Karthik Jewellers. We retain account information to provide your plan and related support and may retain records needed for payments or other applicable obligations.
